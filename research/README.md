# Key papers

The two papers our camera navigator builds on. They are linked, not copied: their licences do not let us redistribute the PDFs.

- Kinnari, Verdoja and Kyrki (2021), *GNSS-denied geolocalization of UAVs by visual matching of onboard camera images with orthophotos*, [arXiv:2103.14381](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14381)
- Kinnari, Verdoja and Kyrki (2022), *Season-invariant GNSS-denied visual localization for UAVs*, [arXiv:2110.01967](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01967)

The full reading list with notes is in [docs/reading-notes.md](../docs/reading-notes.md).
