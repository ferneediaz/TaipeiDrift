Taipei Drift pitch, offline copy
================================

Open index.html in Chrome or Safari. No internet is needed: the fonts, the pictures and the video are in the assets folder.

Keys
  right arrow, space, Page Down, Enter   next slide (a presenter clicker works)
  left arrow, Page Up, Backspace         previous slide
  F                                      full screen on and off
  N                                      speaker notes on and off (with the slide number)
  B                                      jump to the Backup divider
  R                                      restart the video
  Home, End                              first and last slide
  click                                  next slide (left third of the screen: previous)

The demo video is slide 5. It starts by itself when you reach the slide, plays once without sound and stops on its last frame.
The copy in the repository carries the 720p version of the video (5 MB) to keep the repository small.
A click on the video pauses and resumes it.

Keep the whole folder together. The page is built from the same slides as the deck at
https://claude.ai/artifact/RLNDUb3edxoLJz9gdJyDdW (state of 4 October 2026); later changes to that deck are not in this copy.
